# Google Brain

Google Brain was Google's deep learning research division, responsible for some of the most influential AI research in the 2010s–2020s. It was later merged with DeepMind to form **Google DeepMind** (2023).

## Role in the Transformer

Google Brain was the primary institutional affiliation behind "Attention Is All You Need" (2017). Key authors affiliated with Google Brain:

- **Ashish Vaswani** ([[ashish-vaswani]]) — lead implementer
- **Noam Shazeer** — proposed [[scaled-dot-product-attention]], [[multi-head-attention]], and parameter-free position representation
- **Łukasz Kaiser** — co-designed tensor2tensor, the codebase used in experiments

Additional authors were from Google Research (Llion Jones, Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit) and the University of Toronto (Aidan N. Gomez).

## Notable Contributions

- Introduced the [[transformer-architecture]] (2017) — the foundation of BERT, GPT, T5, and virtually all modern LLMs
- Developed tensor2tensor — the training framework used in the original Transformer experiments
