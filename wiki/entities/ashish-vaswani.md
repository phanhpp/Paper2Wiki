# Ashish Vaswani

Ashish Vaswani is a machine learning researcher, affiliated with [[google-brain]] at the time of the Transformer paper. He is the lead author of "Attention Is All You Need" (2017), the paper that introduced the [[transformer-architecture]].

## Contributions

- Designed and implemented the first Transformer models (with Illia Polosukhin)
- Crucially involved in every aspect of the Transformer work
- All authors had equal contribution; listing order was random
